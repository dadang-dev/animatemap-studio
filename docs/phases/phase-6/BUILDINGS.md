Verified source: City of Vancouver's [Building footprints 2009](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/building-footprints-2009/) metadata explicitly defines `hgt_agl` as building-element height above grade in metres, with original Polygon geometry and stable `id`/`bldgid`. It is generalized 2009 LiDAR reference data, not current architecture. The metadata declares the [Open Government Licence – Vancouver](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/pages/licence/), which permits commercial reuse with attribution. Research rejected unsuitable shortcuts: DC's official building-footprint layer has no height field; the similarly named dcgis.org 2016 service is Eastern Nebraska, not District of Columbia. For later historical work, [CShapes' official dataset license](https://icr.ethz.ch/data/cshapes/) is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 despite its R software's GPL listing; do not bundle it as unrestricted commercial geography.
