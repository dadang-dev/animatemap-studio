Risks: real snapshots differ substantially in vertex counts and boundary correspondence; direct index interpolation is unsafe. Accurate source retention and bounded topology checks take priority over enabling every morph. A morph is a visual effect, not reconstructed historical geography; even individually valid feature rings do not establish gap-free multi-region political partitions. Declare any such limitation clearly and reject unsupported cases rather than fabricating topology or geography.
