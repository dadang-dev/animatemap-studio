# Engineering rules

Follow Phase 0–5 gates sequentially. Do not substitute fake rendering for export. Keep evaluator pure and shared by preview/export. Validate JSON with Zod; preserve geographic provenance, islands, and Unicode. Update phase notes with commands and evidence. Do not commit secrets or generated video.

For video work, also read the repository-root [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md). It records the user's established workflow and the validated lessons from population, area, neighbouring-conflict and NATO videos, including voice/timing permissions, flags/callouts and final media verification. Keep historical phase evidence intact and update the root guide when a new reusable lesson is learned.
