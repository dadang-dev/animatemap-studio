# AnimateMap Studio P0

The original `PLAN_CODEX_ANIMATEMAP_STUDIO.md` is the authoritative product plan. Audience: educational/video creators, travel storytellers and location marketing. Desktop-first, English editor with Vietnamese Unicode text.

Required journeys: Vietnam travel route with camera, pins/title and MP4; Southeast Asia country highlights; portrait zoom with marker/text. The implemented four layer types are countryHighlight, route, marker and text. Three authored styles use local public-domain Natural Earth geometry. Exports are fixed-frame Chromium captures and actual FFmpeg encoding.

P1/P2 features remain deferred: scene crossfades, audio, detailed street routing/geocoding, data-driven maps, 3D, historical borders and AI. Local validation evidence and remaining limits are tracked in `phases` and `ACCEPTANCE.md`.
