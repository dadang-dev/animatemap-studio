# Sa Pa terrain snapshot

This pack retains original Mapzen/Tilezen Terrain Tiles from the public AWS bucket. The catalog records exact source URLs, retrieval time and original PNG SHA-256. No source pixels are changed. Tiles use Terrarium encoding; regional sampling is approximately 280 metres per source pixel near Sa Pa, not a new high-resolution survey. Negative/source anomalous values are retained. Heights are for geographic visualization, not navigation, surveying or engineering measurements.

Source distribution and terms: [AWS Terrain Tiles](https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/), [Mapzen rights](https://www.mapzen.com/rights/), [provider attribution and underlying source notices](https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md), [encoding](https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/formats.md).

The regional land data uses global SRTM/GMTED2010 and possible ETOPO1 fallback from US government sources, as inferred from the distributor's source coverage; PNG pixels do not carry a per-pixel survey/source ID. Retain source notices rather than asserting a single new survey license or a current measurement date. The distributor documents USGS public-domain datasets and NOAA's copyright/use notices, including their outside-US language and non-navigation limitations. No claim of endorsement is made.
