# Authored model geometry

The stylized aircraft v1 generated by `apps/web/src/modelImport.ts` is original AnimateMap geometry dedicated under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). It contains 42 vertices and 64 triangles: a body/nose, wings, tailplane and fin. Coordinates declare nominal metres, Z up and +Y forward. The model is illustrative and does not represent a measured aircraft or a factual flight path.

The importer records the SHA-256 of actual OBJ bytes before declared axis/origin conversion. The built-in source credit is “Stylized aircraft · AnimateMap · CC0”. Local user models require explicit source, author, license, credit, units, axes and placement origin; their rights declaration is retained without asserting that AnimateMap owns or independently verifies the supplied asset.

Feet-based and other deliberately malformed inputs in tests are authored fixtures, not factual aviation data. No external model/material/image service is contacted.
